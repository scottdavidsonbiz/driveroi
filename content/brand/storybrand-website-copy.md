# Website Homepage Copy (StoryBrand Framework)

## Hero Section

**Headline:**
Your pipeline depends on referrals.
It doesn't have to.

**Subhead:**
We build GTM systems for B2B companies so revenue becomes predictable.

**CTA Button:** Book a Discovery Call
**Transitional CTA:** Get a Free GTM Health Check

---

## Problem Section (The Stakes)

**Section Header:** Sound familiar?

You've got tools. Maybe a rep or two. Deals come in, but you can't explain why.

- Pipeline is unpredictable. Good months, then silence.
- Referrals carry the business but you can't scale on referrals.
- You've bought CRM, outbound tools, analytics. Nothing connects.
- You're still the primary seller and there's no system for anyone else to follow.

**The cost:** Every month without a system is revenue left on the table, deals lost to stalling, and a business that can't scale past you.

---

## Guide Section (Empathy + Authority)

**Section Header:** We've been in your seat.

We come from FP&A and revenue operations, not marketing. We obsess over the metrics your board asks about: pipeline coverage, conversion velocity, CAC payback, forecast accuracy.

We've built GTM systems for B2B companies across SaaS, industrial services, cybersecurity, and professional services. Every system we build still runs after we leave.

---

## Plan Section (3 Steps)

**Section Header:** How it works

**Step 1: Audit**
We look at your pipeline, sales process, conversion data, and tech stack. You get a report with specific findings. Takes 2 weeks.

**Step 2: Build**
Pipeline architecture, automated outbound, deal stage governance, forecasting, revenue dashboards. Everything integrated. Weeks 3-8.

**Step 3: Run**
Your team runs it. We hand off everything. Optional light-touch retainer for ongoing optimization.

---

## Success Section (Transformation)

**Section Header:** What you walk away with

- A pipeline system that generates qualified opportunities without you
- Outbound sequences that run on autopilot
- Revenue dashboards your board will actually read
- A sales process your next hire can follow on day one
- Infrastructure that outlasts our engagement

---

## Failure Section (Stakes - subtle)

**Section Header:** The alternative

Keep relying on referrals that dry up at the worst time. Hire a full-time ops person at $130K who takes 6 months to ramp. Or keep buying tools and hoping they connect themselves.

---

## CTA Section (Bottom)

**Headline:**
Your pipeline doesn't have to depend on you.

**CTA Button:** Book a Discovery Call
**Subtext:** 30 minutes. We audit your setup and give you specific recommendations. No pitch, just value.

---

## Notes for Framer Implementation

- Hero section: dark background, large headline, two CTAs (primary + transitional)
- Problem section: light background, bullet cards or icons
- Guide section: keep short. 2-3 sentences. Maybe team photos.
- Plan section: 3-column or numbered steps. Purple accent.
- Success section: checkmark list or card grid
- Failure section: keep subtle. Not fear-mongering. Just honest.
- Final CTA: dark background, centered, single button

### StoryBrand Wireframe Structure
The book recommends this exact page flow: Header (CTA) > Stakes > Value Prop > Guide > Plan > Explanatory Paragraph > CTA. The layout above follows this with minor adaptations.

### Key Principle
Never lead with "We do X." Always lead with "You have this problem." The customer scrolls past anything that isn't about them.
