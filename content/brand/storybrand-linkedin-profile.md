## About Section

Most B2B founders I talk to are in the same spot.

They've got a CRM. Maybe Clay or HubSpot. A rep or two. Some sequences running.

But pipeline still feels unpredictable. Good months happen, then nothing. Referrals carry the business but you can't scale on referrals.

The tools aren't the problem. The system is.

I spent years in FP&A and revenue operations (AvalonBay, 6sense) obsessing over the metrics boards actually care about: pipeline coverage, conversion velocity, CAC payback, forecast accuracy.

Now I build GTM infrastructure for B2B companies doing $1M-$25M.

What that looks like:
- Signal-based prospecting that targets the right accounts at the right time
- Outbound systems that run without you
- Pipeline architecture with clear stage governance
- Revenue dashboards your board will actually read

The engagement is simple: we audit your current setup, build the system, and hand it off. Fixed sprints, not open retainers. Everything we build, you own.

If your pipeline depends on you personally selling, or on referrals you can't control, that's the problem I solve.
