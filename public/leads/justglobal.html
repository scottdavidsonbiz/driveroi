Signal Window (Jan-Mar 2026)
Why this matters: New CMOs review their agency roster within the first 120 days. They inherit relationships they didn't choose and budgets they didn't set. The window between appointment and first agency review is when outreach converts highest. These 12 companies are in that window right now.

Methodology

  • Sources: BusinessWire, PR Newswire, company newsrooms, Ad Age CMO Tracker, LinkedIn executive announcements
  • Signal: New CMO or equivalent marketing leader (SVP Marketing, Head of Marketing) appointed January 1 - March 31, 2026
  • ICP filter: B2B companies only. Excluded B2C brands (Chick-fil-A, Saks, Supergoop, J.M. Smucker, Jack in the Box), government-only (USAA), and consumer tech
  • Signal strength: Very High = enterprise B2B with press release + CMO has agency-side or scaled marketing org background. High = B2B with confirmed appointment. Medium = confirmed via LinkedIn, limited press coverage.
