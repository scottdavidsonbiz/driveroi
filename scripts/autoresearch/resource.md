# Cold Email Optimization - Reference

## Target Audience for This Campaign
B2B SaaS companies in the US, 50-500 employees, that are actively hiring SDR/BDR roles. The signal: if they're hiring manual outbound reps, they could be automating instead.

## What Makes Interested Reply Rate Go Up

### Lead Quality (highest leverage)
- Tighter targeting = higher relevance = higher interested rate
- Decision-makers reply more than individual contributors
- Companies in active growth mode (hiring, funding) are more receptive
- Smaller companies (50-200) reply more than larger ones

### Subject Lines
- Lowercase, under 4 words
- Question format adds ~15% open lift
- Specific to their situation, not generic

### Body Copy
- First line about THEM, not you
- Under 80 words
- One CTA, question format, not a meeting ask
- No em dashes, no jargon, no exclamation marks
- Line breaks between every 1-2 sentences

### What This Campaign Tests
Whether companies actively hiring SDRs respond to the premise that signal-based automation can replace or augment manual headcount. The copy should reference their specific hiring activity without being creepy.

## Mutation Strategy
Levers to pull (highest leverage first):
1. Subject line - different hook
2. Opening line - different angle on their SDR hiring
3. Value prop framing - automation vs augmentation vs ROI
4. CTA - different ask
5. Body length - shorter vs longer
6. Tone - casual vs professional

## What NOT to Mutate
- email_gap (10 min)
- daily_limit (125/campaign)
- Number of steps (1 - single email only)
- Sending schedule (8am-5pm ET, weekdays)
- link_tracking: off
- open_tracking: off
