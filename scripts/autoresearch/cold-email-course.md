# Cold Email Knowledge Base

## Cannonball Method (DriveROI's framework)
- 3 blocks: Situation, Insight, Inquisition
- Email 1: no "I/we", CTA is a question not a meeting ask
- Zero instances of "we/our/I" in the email (except sign-off)
- No solution described. No case studies leading.
- Subject: 2-5 words, lowercase

## What Works (from DriveROI's data)
- Signal relevance matters more than copy quality
- The TWO outbound conversions (Compliancy Group, Dexory) both happened because the prospect was in an active buying window
- Compliancy Group: converted because breach data was relevant to THEIR business
- Dexory: new BDR leader in month 1, building from scratch
- 31K emails sent across all campaigns, <1% reply rate overall
- Only HHS Breach campaign worked (1.0% reply, generated a real client)

## DriveROI Tone Rules
- No parroting (never repeat what someone said back to them)
- No name-dropping to prove listening
- No flattery disguised as insight
- No punchy closers or mic-drop one-liners
- No proving how smart/attentive we are
- Write to inform, not to impress
- Default tone: direct, professional, low ego

## Subject Line Formulas
- Type 1: Possessive + Topic ("your Q1 pipeline")
- Type 2: Question Format ("RevOps question")
- Type 3: Segment + Topic ("Series B RevOps")
- 3-7 words, 30-50 characters max
- Lowercase always

## What NOT to Do
- No em dashes
- No "congrats on the new role"
- No "I noticed that..."
- No "we help companies like..."
- No rhetorical questions except the CTA
- No fake urgency or scarcity
- No links in cold email (hurts deliverability)
